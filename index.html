Sentence

The part of a text that comes immediately before a sentence-ending punctuation mark is called a sentence.

Sentence ending punctuation হলো তিনটি:

.Full stop
ফুলস্টপ
?Question mark
প্রশ্নবোধক
!Exclamation
আশ্চর্যবোধক

Types of sentences

There are three types of sentences based on the structure of a sentence.

1. Simple Sentence

যেই বাক্যে মাত্র একটা Independent Clause থাকবে এবং কোনো Dependent Clause থাকবে না, সেটা Simple Sentence।

Example: I like tea.

2. Compound Sentence

কোনো Dependent Clause থাকবে না, কিন্তু Independent Clause-এর সংখ্যা একের বেশি হবে।

Example: I like tea, and she likes coffee.

3. Complex Sentence

Dependent Clause থাকলেই সেটা Complex Sentence হবে। Complex Sentence-এ Independent Clause একটাও থাকতে পারে, একের বেশিও থাকতে পারে।

Example: I like tea because it is warm.

Clause

A clause is a part of a sentence that can also be used as a complete sentence.

Clauses are basically of two types:

Independent Clause
Dependent Clause
Independent Clause চেনার উপায়: যদি কোনো Clause-কে সহজভাবে কেটে নিয়ে একটা ফুলস্টপ দিয়ে বসিয়ে দেওয়া যায়, তবে তাকে Independent Clause বলে।

Three types of Dependent Clause

Adjective Clause
Adverb Clause
Noun Clause

Clause is the head of the sentence

As we see, a sentence is basically controlled by different clauses, so a clause is called the head of the sentence.

When a clause is reduced, it turns into a phrase. Unfortunately, a phrase has no control over the structure of the sentence.
